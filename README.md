OdakKampüs ⏱️

🔗 Live App: https://yusufakn16.github.io/FocusCampus/

A lightweight, privacy-focused study timer and productivity tracker. It runs 100% in your browser—no login or server required, and every user's data stays completely private on their own device.

🎯 What It Does

Flexible Timing: Choose between a custom countdown timer (set any minutes) or an open-ended stopwatch.

One-Click Subject Selection: Save frequent subjects with + Ekle and select them instantly from quick-access badges.

Visual Analytics: Daily goal progress bars, 7-day bar charts, and subject breakdown doughnut charts.

100% Private: All study logs are stored strictly on your own computer (localStorage). Different people opening the same link never see each other's data.

Data Backup: Export or restore your study history anytime via .json or .csv.

🚀 How to Use

Open the App: Go to https://yusufakn16.github.io/FocusCampus/

Choose Mode: Select ⏳ Süre Belirle (Countdown) or ⏱️ Kronometre (Stopwatch).

Pick a Topic: Type a subject and click + Ekle, or click any saved subject pill.

Track & Save: Click ▶️ Sayacı Başlat. When finished, hit 💾 Bitir & Kaydet to log your study minutes and rate your focus.

🛠️ Tech Stack

Pure HTML5, Vanilla JavaScript, Tailwind CSS (CDN), and Chart.js (CDN). Zero installation, zero dependencies.
